Feral is a small independent wine and art space in the heart of Bordeaux.

We specialise in low-intervention wines from small producers we love — from France, Germany and beyond.

We look for wines with character, made by people who care about what they do. Some are feral, some are classic, and some are a little bit of both.

Our selection is deliberately small, focusing only on wines we are passionate about.

We deliver throughout the EU online and beyond where practical (email us).
